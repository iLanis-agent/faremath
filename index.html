Fare Math

The pass never loses money. You do, at ride 31.

Boundary: counting arithmetic only Fare Math divides your pass price by your ride price and counts your rides. It never tells you whether transit is worth it - it only prices the pass against the rides you actually take.

The break-even truth

The $86.00 pass against $2.75 rides breaks even at ride 32. Ride 33 onward is free - and ride 31 is where most months end.

The month truth

22 rides at $2.75 are $60.50 - the $86.00 pass costs $25.50 more than the rides. 40 rides are $110.00, and the pass saves $24.00.

The commute truth

2 rides a day for 20 days is 40 rides - count the vacation days honestly, because the pass already did.

Run your numbers
Fare Math is exact arithmetic over your pass price, your ride price, and your ride count - nothing more.
